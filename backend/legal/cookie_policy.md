# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** June 9, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how HireIQ uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and application.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. We use cookies to enable secure authentication sessions, remember recruiter preferences, and collect anonymous system metrics.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### A. Strictly Necessary Cookies
These cookies are required for the application to function:
- **Authentication Session**: We store a local JWT token in browser headers/session to authenticate user API requests.
- **CSRF Tokens**: Used to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks on forms.

### B. Functional & Preference Cookies
- We use local storage to persist interface settings, such as current workspace selected, sorting weights, and UI dark/light mode preferences.

### C. Analytical & Performance Cookies
- **PostHog**: We use third-party analytics cookies to track signup funnels, platform feature usage (e.g. clicking the bias audit button), and subscription conversion rates. These cookies do not collect candidate PII.

---

## 3. Controlling Your Cookies
Most browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences (block, delete, or limit). However, if you disable strictly necessary cookies, the HireIQ application will not be able to authenticate your recruiter sessions, and you will not be able to log in.
